# Broadband basics

## What is broadband?
Broadband is an always-on internet connection with enough capacity for web browsing, video calls, streaming, downloads, and connected devices. The experience depends on the plan speed, Wi-Fi conditions, device capability, and the number of people using the connection at once.

## Download, upload, and latency
Download speed affects streaming and downloads. Upload speed affects video calls, cloud backups, and sharing files. Latency is the delay between a device and an online service; lower latency generally helps real-time gaming and calls.

## FTTH and fiber broadband
Fiber-to-the-home (FTTH) brings an optical-fiber cable close to or into the premises. Fiber is generally well suited to high speeds, stable multi-device use, and lower latency than older copper-based access technologies.

## Data usage and concurrent users
Video quality, game downloads, cloud backups, and the number of simultaneous users all affect broadband demand. A speed tier is a capacity guideline, not a guarantee of every website's performance.
