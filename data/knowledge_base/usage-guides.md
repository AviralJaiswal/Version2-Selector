# Usage guides

## Gaming
Online gaming benefits from stable connectivity and low latency. A wired connection, a capable router, and avoiding background downloads can be important. Higher broadband tiers provide more shared capacity but cannot guarantee latency to a specific game server.

## Streaming
HD and 4K streaming need sustained bandwidth. Multiple simultaneous streams, other household activity, Wi-Fi signal quality, and the streaming provider all influence playback quality.

## Work from home
Video conferencing, VPN use, cloud synchronization, and collaboration tools benefit from stable upload and download performance. Ethernet or strong 5 GHz Wi-Fi near the router can improve call reliability.

## Smart homes and many devices
Smart speakers, cameras, TVs, phones, and laptops share the connection. A higher tier and a well-designed Wi-Fi layout can provide more capacity and coverage for multiple simultaneous workloads.
