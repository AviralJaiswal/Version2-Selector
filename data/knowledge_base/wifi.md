# Wi-Fi and home networking

## Router and ONT
An ONT converts the incoming optical-fiber signal for home networking. A router shares that connection with devices through Wi-Fi and Ethernet. In some installations these functions are in one device.

## Wi-Fi 5 and Wi-Fi 6
Wi-Fi 5 is based on 802.11ac. Wi-Fi 6 is based on 802.11ax and is designed to improve efficiency in homes with many connected devices. Wi-Fi 6 does not by itself increase the broadband plan speed, and devices must support it to benefit fully.

## Ethernet and mesh Wi-Fi
Ethernet is a wired connection and can provide a more consistent link for gaming, desktop workstations, or speed testing. Mesh Wi-Fi uses multiple coordinated access points to improve coverage in larger or difficult-to-cover homes.

## Better Wi-Fi coverage
Place the router in an open, central location where practical. Avoid enclosing it in cabinets or placing it near major sources of interference. Use the 5 GHz band near the router for higher performance; use 2.4 GHz where longer range is more important.
