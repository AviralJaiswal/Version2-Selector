# Existing Customer Support Knowledge Base

## Add-on Services
Existing customers can add optional services to an active connection without taking a new order.
- **Wi-Fi 6 Mesh Add-on (ADD-MESH)**: ₹199/month. Extra mesh nodes for whole-home coverage and dead-zone removal in large or multi-floor homes.
- **Static IP Add-on (ADD-STATIC-IP)**: ₹299/month. Dedicated IPv4 for CCTV remote access, hosted services, or gaming servers.
- **OTT Boost Add-on**: ₹149/month. Adds extra streaming apps when the current plan's OTT bundle is not enough.
- **Parental Control Add-on**: ₹99/month. App/site scheduling and device-level filters on the Data Shopper router.
- **Additional TV / Set-top**: ₹149/month per extra point after the first ONT.
Add-ons start from the next billing cycle after confirmation. They can be removed with 7 days' notice; unused days in the current month are not refunded.

## Billing, Invoice & Payments
- Monthly charges are billed on the plan activation anniversary date.
- GST invoice is emailed to the registered email and available in the account after payment.
- Failed auto-pay: retry the same card/UPI within 3 days to avoid a temporary speed cap.
- Plan upgrades are prorated for the remaining days of the cycle. Downgrades apply from the next billing cycle.
- Duplicate charges: share the registered number and transaction ID; reversal is typically 5–7 business days after verification.

## Troubleshooting Slow Speed & Drops
- Connect a laptop by Ethernet to the ONT and run a speed test. Wi-Fi is often slower than the subscribed Mbps.
- Use the 5 GHz SSID (`DataShopper_5G`) for streaming and calls; 2.4 GHz is for range only.
- Power-cycle: unplug ONT for 30 seconds, then wait 2–3 minutes for PON to go solid green.
- **PON solid green**: fiber link healthy. **LOS flashing red**: fiber signal loss — reseat the yellow patch cord; if it continues, book a technician.
- Restart mesh nodes after the main ONT. Persistent packet loss after Ethernet testing needs a line check, not a plan change.

## Support Request & Technician Visit
- 24/7 support via this assistant, in-app chat, or **customer.support@qcom.com**.
- Remote line test first (optical power typically -15 dBm to -25 dBm).
- Technician visit when LOS stays red, Ethernet speed stays far below the plan, or indoor wiring is damaged.
- Standard visit: next working day. Metro express: same day if logged before 12:00 PM.
- Keep the registered mobile reachable; the engineer calls 30 minutes before arrival.

## Connection Relocation & Temporary Hold
- Shift to a new address: verify the new complete street address. If the PIN is serviceable, a relocation order is raised; installation follows standard 24–48 hour timelines.
- Temporary vacation hold: up to 30 days per year; billing pauses after the current cycle; reactivation needs 24 hours' notice.

## Plan Change Rules For Existing Customers
- Upgrade: higher Mbps in the same telecom circle; prorated from confirmation.
- Downgrade: lower Mbps in the same circle; takes effect next billing cycle.
- Current plan stays active until the change is confirmed.
- Region is taken from the stored service address / order, not from a new PIN check.
