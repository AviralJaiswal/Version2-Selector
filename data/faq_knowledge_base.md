## Troubleshooting Slow Internet, High Latency & Connection Drops
- **Slow Speed over Wi-Fi**: 
  - Ensure your device is connected to the **5GHz Wi-Fi band** (indicated as `SignalSelector_5G`) for maximum speed. 2.4GHz is meant for range and caps out around 50-70 Mbps due to channel congestion.
  - Test speeds using an Ethernet LAN cable connected directly to the ONT router for accurate speed verification.
- **ONT Router LED Indicators**:
  - **PON Light Solid Green**: Fiber optical link is healthy and connected to ISP node.
  - **LOS Light Flashing Red**: Optical Fiber cable connection is broken or signal loss exceeds -28 dBm. Check physical yellow fiber patch cord for tight connection. If problem persists, request technician visit.
  - **LAN / Wi-Fi Lights**: Blinking indicates active data transmission.
- **Quick Power Cycle Reset**: Unplug the ONT power adapter for 30 seconds, then plug back in. Allow 2 minutes for optical link synchronization.
- **High Ping / Packet Loss in Gaming**: Connect via Cat6 LAN cable, ensure no background torrents or heavy downloads are running, and set DNS to 1.1.1.1 or 8.8.8.8.
