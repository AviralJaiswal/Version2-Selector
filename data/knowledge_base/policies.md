# Prototype policy information

## Policy scope
This document contains general prototype guidance, not an authoritative contract. Current terms, exact commitments, fees, refunds, and promotions must be confirmed by an authoritative policy or billing system.

## Appointments and installation
Installation can require access, building permissions, and a suitable equipment location. Appointment changes should be made through the appointment service, which is the authority for availability.

## Plan changes and equipment
Plan changes may depend on serviceability, equipment capability, and the current product catalog. Equipment should be handled according to the provider's supplied instructions; support can help with account-specific issues.

## Payments, cancellation, and refunds
Payment status must be checked through the payment service. Cancellation and refund outcomes can depend on activation, installation progress, applicable terms, and the account record. This prototype does not state exact refund amounts or contractual commitments.
