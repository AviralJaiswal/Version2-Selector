# Installation

## Technician visit
After an address is qualified and an appointment is selected, a technician typically assesses the route for the fiber cable, installs or connects the optical network terminal (ONT), and helps verify router and Wi-Fi operation. Actual appointment availability always comes from the Appointment API.

## Site readiness
Keep access available to the premises and a suitable power outlet near the preferred equipment location. If possible, decide where Wi-Fi coverage is most important and tell the technician about walls, floors, or rooms that may need coverage.

## During installation
The technician may route fiber to the installation point, connect the ONT/router, confirm indicator lights, configure Wi-Fi, and perform basic connectivity checks. Building permissions, civil work, or an unsuitable cable route can affect timing.

## Customer presence
An adult who can provide access and confirm equipment placement is generally helpful during installation. For changing or rescheduling an appointment, use the authoritative appointment service rather than relying on general guidance.
